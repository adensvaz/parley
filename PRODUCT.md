# Parley — what it does, and how it flows

## In one line
Parley listens to both sides of a live cold call and tells the rep exactly what to say next.

## The problem
Cold callers freeze. The prospect says *"I'm not interested"* and the rep's mind goes blank.
Scripts don't help — real calls don't follow scripts. Ramping a new rep takes months.

## What Parley does
A small always-on panel beside the rep's dialer. During the call it:

1. **Hears the objection** — transcribes both sides in real time.
2. **Hands over the line** — the proven rebuttal appears the instant the prospect pushes back (~100ms, because likely objections are pre-computed while the rep is talking).
3. **Coaches live** — talk/listen ratio, "you're talking too much", stage of the call, how warm the lead is getting.
4. **Writes it up** — after the call: score, summary, CRM log, drafted follow-up.
5. **Gets smarter** — it learns which rebuttal actually books appointments, per market, and serves that one more often.

## The three flows

### Flow 1 — Setup (once)
```
Sign in → pick your playbook → connect your dialer/CRM → ready
```
**Playbooks** load different objections, scripts and compliance rules:
Expired Listings · FSBO · Circle Prospecting · Buyer Follow-up · Investor · B2B SDR

### Flow 2 — The call (the product)
```
Dial  →  lead context appears  →  they object  →  rebuttal card  →  coach nudges  →  close
```
| On screen | When |
|---|---|
| Who you're calling (name, address, history) | on connect |
| Opener line | on connect |
| 🛡️ Objection + exact rebuttal | the moment they push back |
| 🎯 Coach nudge ("ask a question") | when you over-talk |
| 📊 Lead temperature / tone read | continuously |
| Call stage bar | intro → discovery → value → objection → close |

### Flow 3 — After the call (automatic)
```
Score → summary → CRM logged → follow-up drafted → next call
```
Manager sees the whole floor: calls, scores, appointments, per rep.

## Who it's for
- **Real estate agents** cold-calling expired listings & FSBOs
- **B2B SDRs** doing outbound
- **Sales managers** who need new reps productive in days, not months

## Why it wins
- **Speed** — the line is on screen before you'd finish panicking.
- **It learns** — a competitor can copy today's best rebuttal, not the system that finds tomorrow's.
- **It's compliant** — DNC, consent and calling-window checks built in.
- **It's discreet** — stays out of your screen-share.
