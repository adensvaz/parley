The idea
The page doesn’t describe Parley.
It is Parley, mid-call.
The hero is a live cold call in progress. The prospect’s words stream in on one side of a center waveform “spine.” Parley’s coaching cards whisper in on the other — exactly what a rep sees over their shoulder.
As you scroll, the call advances through its stages — Opener → Objection → Discovery → Close — and every section is a moment where Parley saves the rep.
