Parley, Inc.01
The problem
Cold calls aren’t lost in the pitch.
They’re lost in the pause.
The moment a prospect pushes back, the rep freezes. Three seconds of dead air, a weak “thanks for your time,” and the deal is gone. Every sales org fights the same four problems:
Reps freeze
The average rep fumbles the objection they’ve heard 1,000 times. Talent doesn’t transfer through a script deck.
Ramp is brutal
New reps take ~2–3 months to get productive. Most churn before they do. That’s pure burned payroll.
Coaching doesn’t scale
Managers hear ~2% of calls. Feedback lands days later, after the same mistake is made 100 more times.
Tools grade too late
Gong-style analytics tell you why you lost — after the call is over and the deal is dead.
Inside sales runs on volume × conversion. Both break at exactly the moment help is impossible to deliver: mid-call.

Why now
Sub-second in-call AI just became possible.
The three things real-time coaching needed all crossed the line in the last 24 months.
Fast, cheap STT
Streaming speech-to-text (Deepgram-class) now transcribes both sides live for pennies — the input layer is finally real-time.
LLMs at latency
Modern models classify an objection and retrieve the right line in <1s — fast enough to matter mid-sentence, not next week.
Sales went remote
Reps dial from softphones on laptops. The overlay has a screen to live on — and managers can’t walk the floor anymore.
The wedgeIncumbents (Gong, Chorus) were built pre-latency — architected for post-call. Real-time is a new category, not a feature they can bolt on.
