# Parley — Content Strategy

**Goal priority:** (1) Demand creation via shareable short-form → (2) searchable authority → (3) leads.
Cold calling is *inherently shareable* — live call audio, objection teardowns, and "watch me set an
appointment in 4 minutes" are dopamine content. We weaponize that. Search is the compounding base layer.

**One-line positioning:** *Parley whispers the perfect line in your ear on every cold call — so you never
freeze, never lose the appointment, and never wing it again.*

---

## The audience truth (voice-of-customer)
What real estate cold callers & SDRs actually say (use this language verbatim in content):
- "I freeze when they hit me with an objection."
- "I make 100 dials and book nothing — I don't know what I'm doing wrong."
- "I hate cold calling / call reluctance is killing me."
- "Expired/FSBO sellers are brutal, they've been called 40 times."
- "My new reps take 6 months to ramp."
- "I can never remember the script when it matters."

Every pillar answers one of these in their words.

---

## 4 Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Objection Handling (the wedge; most shareable)
The product *is* objection handling. Own this category.
- Hub: "The Cold Call Objection Handbook" (every objection + the exact rebuttal).
- Spokes: "I'm not interested" · "We already have an agent" · "Just send me an email" ·
  "How'd you get my number" · "I'm going to sell it myself (FSBO)" · "No budget" · "Call me later."
- Format: 30–60s vertical video — *objection on screen → Parley's rebuttal → rep says it → appointment*.

### Pillar 2 — Live Call Teardowns (authority + proof)
- "I cold called 10 expired listings with AI in my ear — here's what happened" (YouTube long-form).
- "Rate my cold call" series: real (consented) recordings, Parley cards overlaid, score the rep.
- Before/after: same rep, same objection, with vs. without Parley.

### Pillar 3 — Prospecting Playbooks by niche (searchable, use-case content)
Formula [persona] + [prospecting type] = long-tail SEO:
- "Expired listing scripts that actually work in 2026"
- "FSBO cold calling script (and the 5 objections you'll always hear)"
- "Circle prospecting scripts for new agents"
- "SDR cold call opener templates" · "Speed-to-lead: calling internet buyer leads"
- Each playbook = free script + "or let Parley run it live" CTA.

### Pillar 4 — Call Reluctance & Ramp (emotional + manager buyer)
- "Why you freeze on cold calls (and the 1 fix)"
- "How to ramp a new SDR in 2 weeks instead of 6 months" (sells to sales managers / team leads).
- "The psychology of the cold call no" — thought leadership.

## Channel plan (where these buyers actually are)

**Real estate side**
- **YouTube** — #1. Agents binge prospecting content (Ricky Carruth, Brandon Mulrenin energy). Long-form teardowns + Shorts.
- **Instagram Reels / TikTok** — objection clips, "POV: the seller says…" skits.
- **Facebook Groups** — Lab Coat Agents, Real Estate Agents (no value-add posts; do live call breakdowns).
- **BiggerPockets** — investor/wholesaler angle.

**SDR / B2B side**
- **LinkedIn** — #1 for SDRs/managers. Objection carousels, ramp-time data posts, founder POV.
- **X/Twitter** — sales-twitter threads, hot takes on cold calling being "dead" (it's not — that's the hook).
- **YouTube** — cold call review channels.

**Owned**
- **Newsletter "The Daily Dial"** — one objection + one rebuttal + one teardown clip, every weekday. Habit loop.
- **Blog** — playbooks + comparison/decision content.

## Lead magnets (capture the demand)
1. **The 40-Objection Cheat Sheet** (printable, sits by the phone) — top of funnel.
2. **Niche script packs** (Expired / FSBO / SDR) — gated, segments by persona.
3. **Free "Call Score" tool** — upload a recording, get an AI scorecard → product wedge.

## Distribution rhythm (one creator can run this)
- Record **1 long-form YouTube teardown / week** → slice into **5–7 Shorts/Reels**.
- **1 LinkedIn post/day** (objection or data), **3 X posts/day**.
- **Newsletter daily** (recycled clip + objection).
- Result: ~40 assets/week from one filming session. Content compounds; objections never run out.

## Why this wins
Objection content is **infinite, evergreen, and emotionally charged**, the product is the literal payoff of
every clip ("…or just let Parley say it for you"), and live-call proof is unfakeable social proof competitors
can't cheaply copy.
