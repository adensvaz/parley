# Parley — Marketing Hooks & Go-To-Market Angles

## The core hook (everything ladders to this)
Cluely went viral on **"AI helps you cheat on everything."** We take the *exact same emotional engine* —
**"AI whispers the perfect thing to say, live"** — and aim it at a moment people are genuinely terrified of:
**the cold call**. The villain isn't a proctor, it's the *blank mind when the prospect pushes back*.

> **Tagline:** *Never freeze on a call again.*
> **Alt taglines:**
> - "The earpiece every top closer wishes they had."
> - "It hears the objection before you panic — and hands you the line."
> - "Your best rep, whispering in every other rep's ear."
> - "Cold calling, but you always know what to say next."
> - "Stop winging it. Start closing it."

## 5 positioning angles (pick per channel/audience)

1. **The anti-freeze angle** (emotional, B2C agents) — "You know the feeling: they say 'I'm not interested'
   and your mind goes blank. Parley already has the comeback on your screen." → call reluctance is the pain.
2. **The instant-ramp angle** (manager/team buyer) — "Make your greenest rep sound like your best one on
   day one. 6-month ramp → 6 days." → sells seats, higher ACV.
3. **The unfair-advantage angle** (status/FOMO) — "Top producers already have a coach in their ear. Now you
   do too." Mirrors Cluely's "everyone's using it, you're behind" energy.
4. **The proof angle** (skeptics) — live, recorded, scored calls. "Don't believe me? Watch."
5. **The legitimacy flip** (PR-proof) — "Cluely got banned from interviews. Parley gets *invited* onto your
   sales floor." Same tech, zero stigma — lean into it publicly.

## Viral / growth mechanics (mapped to the idea library)

### #41 Short-form video — THE primary growth engine
Objection-handling clips are infinite, evergreen, emotional, and the product is the literal punchline.
- Format lock: **objection appears on screen → Parley card → rep says it → "appointment set."**
- Hook lines (first 1.5s): *"He said 'just send me an email' — watch."* / *"This objection ends 90% of calls.
  Here's the comeback."* / *"POV: AI is feeding you the perfect line."*
- **How to start:** film one teardown session, cut 7 clips, post daily. **Outcome:** the demo IS the ad.

### #15 Engineering as marketing — "Cold Call Score" free tool
Upload a call recording → AI scorecard (talk ratio, objections missed, what to say instead). Free, viral,
self-segmenting, and it's the product wedge. Reps share their scores; managers run their team's calls.
- **Start:** ship the uploader against the existing `copilot.ts` engine. **Outcome:** top-of-funnel + email capture.

### #93 Viral loop — "Powered by Parley" on every win
Auto-generated post-call wins ("Appointment set in 3:42 🔥") + a shareable call-score card watermark.
Plus a **referral**: give a rep a free month for every teammate who installs — sales floors install as a pack.

### #87 Powered-by / #11 Comparison pages (SEO + intent capture)
- Comparison/SEO pages: "Parley vs cold call scripts," "Parley vs Balto," "best AI cold calling assistant 2026,"
  "expired listing objection handler." High commercial intent, low competition in real-estate niche.

### #38 Reddit + community marketing
r/realtors, r/sales, r/RealEstate, BiggerPockets, Lab Coat Agents — NOT ads. Post genuine teardowns and the
free score tool. Let the product do the talking.

### #79 Waitlist referrals + #78 Product Hunt launch
- Pre-launch waitlist with referral skip-the-line (Parley merch + free months).
- PH launch angle: *"Cluely for cold calls — the AI that tells you what to say when they say no."*

## Launch narrative (the story the press/founders repeat)
*"Cluely proved people will install an AI that whispers what to say in real time — it just pointed it at the
wrong, sketchy use case. We took the same engine and gave it to the people who need it most and have nothing
to hide: salespeople on cold calls. Same magic, real job, no shame."*

This is your founder POV post, your PH tagline, your podcast-tour one-liner. It's controversial enough to
travel (#unconventional) and clean enough to scale.
