## Deliverability + cadence rules
- Real from-name ("Aden at Parley"), real reply-to. Founder tone on everything early.
- Behavior beats calendar: never send "make your first call" to someone who already did.
- One CTA per email. Plain text > heavy HTML for the founder emails (better inbox placement + feels human).
- Localize the win-back / expansion for MENA (Arabic subject variants, AED framing on ROI math).
